Who I am

I'm a Data Analyst and Python Developer based in Gurugram, India, currently pursuing my MCA (AI & ML specialisation) at K.R. Mangalam University. My work sits at the intersection of analytics and engineering — I build things that process data and build things that display it clearly.

My toolkit covers the full analytics lifecycle: from raw extraction and cleaning with Pandas and NumPy, through exploratory analysis and visualisation with Matplotlib, to dashboards in Power BI and Tableau. On the engineering side I work with Python REST APIs, MongoDB, SQL, and the MERN stack for full-stack builds.

I'm particularly interested in finance automation, reporting pipelines, and process optimisation — areas where clean data and good tooling make a measurable difference.
